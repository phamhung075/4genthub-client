# The room's budget safeguard parks a seat that crosses its ceiling

### Added
- `internal/roombudget/`: the room's budget in one place - the limits file (`rooms/<room>/limits.json`: `enabled`, `usd_per_seat_24h`, `calls_per_seat_min`, written 0600 through a temporary file and a rename), the measurement of one seat (the USD its assistant turns cost inside 24h, read from the same `.message.usage.cost.total` `room report` reads, and the tool calls its guard log recorded inside the minute), the breach decision, and the park record (`<seat dir>/parked.json`). Every limit SHIPS OFF, and a room with no file enforces nothing.
- `internal/clientlifecycle/budgetguard.go`: `4genteam budget-run --room RIG`, the per-room safeguard `agent` launches beside `idle-run`. It lists the room's RUNNING seats from the runtime, measures each one, and on a breach stops the seat (StopSeat), writes the park record and files ONE rail row - from `budget@<room>` to the lead's session - naming the seat and the number. The call ceiling must hold for two passes; the USD ceiling parks on the first. A stop the runtime refused is neither recorded nor filed, and a room whose seats cannot be listed measures nothing rather than guessing. It heartbeats as `budget`, so `doctor` tells a working guard from a dead one.
- `internal/clientroom/limits.go`: `4genteam room limits ROOM [--usd N] [--calls N] [--on|--off]`. With no flag it prints the limits in force and writes nothing; with one it writes the file, keeping the numbers it does not name, and a file that does not exist yet starts at the room's agreed 25 USD/24h and 300 calls/min. Zero is refused - OFF is how a room says it has no ceiling - and so is `--on` with `--off`.
- `internal/clientroom/room.go`: the room accepts a `limits` sub-verb (its dispatch, its usage and its entry in the room's own verb table). The park's rendering on a seat's row is NOT here: it belongs to the park-semantics lane (`internal/clientroom/park.go`'s `parkNote`, which reads the same record through `roombudget.ReadParked` and prints it for `status` and `reseat` alike, so one renderer serves both verbs).

### Changed
- `internal/clientservices/services.go`, `internal/clientlifecycle/agent.go`: the safeguard registry, the report's rows and agent's child list carry the budget guard (threshold 45 s, three times its 15 s pass), and the four pinning tests of those lists name it.
- `internal/clientlifecycle/budgetguard.go`: the park's ORDER is the contract, both ways, because the RECORD is the park - every client route that refuses a parked seat work does it by reading that file. The record is written FIRST and the seat is only stopped once it exists; a record that could not be written leaves the seat RUNNING, files nothing and is retried on the next pass (stopping it anyway left a seat that no route refuses and nobody can explain, and nothing retried it). A record that landed stands whatever the stop does next: a refused `StopSeat` is logged and the row is still filed. A seat that already carries a record is left alone, so a guard that restarted does not stop and file the same seat twice. The row names the RECORD as the way back (`<seat dir>/parked.json`, and says outright that a bare reseat restarts a seat that is still refused work) instead of sending the reader to a reseat.
