# A parked seat is not handed work

### Added
- `internal/seatpark`: the ONE refusal a parked seat gets from a client route that would hand it work, and nil when it may take work. It reads the breaker's own record (`roombudget.ReadParked`) and says what stopped it - the reason, the cost, the calls, the time and the record's path - so a sender can tell raising the ceiling apart from leaving the seat down. `StopSeat` alone left a park cosmetic: the seat is down, and the next `send` or `continue` is what brings it back to cross the ceiling again.
- The four client routes that hand a seat work ask it: `4genteam send` in both shapes (`internal/clientseat/reseat.go`), the restore brief and the room revive (`internal/clientrestore/restore.go`). A brief withheld from a parked seat counts as NOT delivered, and a revive leaves the seat down and NAMES it, because a seat this verb deliberately left down is not a seat that came up.
- `4genteam room status` and `room reseat` rows carry the park as a trailing segment, in the one rendering both verbs already share (`seatRow`/`parkNote`). A row that showed only `stopped` left the reader to guess between an idle seat and a parked one; the park is read from the record, not inferred from the state word. The segment is `parked=<RFC3339> reason="<sentence>" cost_usd=<n> calls=<n>`: the reason is quoted because it is the breaker's own sentence and the row's fields are space-separated. `48fc6a8` took the breaker's own `parkedNote` out of `room.go` and handed this rendering to this lane, so `parkNote` is the only one and it is reached from `seatRow`, where the status and the reseat rows cannot drift apart.

### Where it speaks, and where it does not
- THE ROOT IS THE STATE ROOT, not the seat store. The record lives at `<state root>/rooms/<room>/seats/<seat>/parked.json` - the directory the seat runs in, which is also its `PI_CODING_AGENT_DIR` - while `clientenv`'s seat store (`~/.config/4genthub/seats`) holds audit lines and pins. A park read out of the store would name a path that never exists and ALLOW every send, silently.
- A send aimed at ANOTHER daemon's socket is not gated here: a park record belongs to the daemon that wrote it, under a root no socket path names, so the gate stands aside rather than answering on the wrong file. The runtime's own `Send` is where that direction belongs, because it is the one process every route to a seat goes through.
